# Robustness plan (phase_05)

**Purpose.** Which additional analyses materially strengthen the project, at what cost,
and what each one may and may not be used to claim. Written 2026-10-22; decision log
D05.06. Nothing here changes the frozen primary benchmark (`primary_v1`).

**Cost basis, measured not estimated.** The primary run computed **250 tasks in about 9.4
minutes of wall clock with 6 workers** (`results/models/primary_v1`: session started
00:30:21 UTC, `run_status.json` updated 00:39:45 UTC), i.e. **about 2.3 s per task at 6
workers**. Every figure below is derived from that rate; tasks dominated by M2 or M2P are
slower than the mix average, so ranges are given rather than single numbers.

---

## 1. Ranked recommendations

| # | Analysis | Scientific question | Tasks | Cost at 6 workers | Status |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---|
| **R1** | **Negative controls, 1000 draws of each null** (`configs/experiment_controls.yaml`) | Is the observed expression advantage larger than what this pipeline produces when expression *cannot* carry signal? Are any gene sets more important than matched random sets? | 250 real + 15,000 control | **~10–20 h** (resumable) | **Strongly recommended.** The single highest-value missing analysis |
| **R2** | **Leave-one-tissue-out** (`configs/experiment_loto.yaml`, `loto_v1`) | Does an expression-only model generalise to a tissue it has never seen? | ~40 | **~2 min** | **Strongly recommended.** Cheapest real generalisation test available |
| **R3** | **Outcome-scale sensitivity** (`configs/experiment_scales.yaml`, `scales_v1`) | Does the advantage survive a different outcome scale (neglog, logit), or is it an artefact of the raw scale? | 400 | **~15 min** | **Strongly recommended** |
| **R4** | **Random forest M3** (`configs/experiment_exploratory_m3.yaml`, `exploratory_m3_v1`) | Does a different model family extract more signal from the same features and splits? | 200 | **~8 min** | Recommended, exploratory only |
| **R5** | **Descriptive re-analyses of saved outputs** (no fitting) | Repeat stability, calibration, tissue-level differences, corrected within-tissue ranking | 0 | seconds | **Already implemented**; regenerated by the standard table and figure commands |

R2, R3 and R4 together cost **under half an hour**. R1 is an overnight job. There is no
reason to run a reduced version of R2–R4.

## 2. R1: what the negative controls can and cannot license

A control draw refits M1 and M2 (permuted-outcome null) or M2 on matched random gene sets
(random-set null), on **repeat 0 only**, five outer folds, with the same splits, seeds,
grids and nested tuning as the real fits. The existing pipeline already consumes the
results: `results/tables/<run_id>/negative_controls.csv` (currently a header with no rows)
gains one ΔMAE per draw, and `explainability/gene_set_tests.csv` gains the p-value and
BH-adjusted columns that are presently empty.

**Legitimate, immediately:**

- A **descriptive comparison** of the observed ΔMAE = 0.0038317 against the null
  distribution of ΔMAE. Report it as the observed value's position within the null
  (for example "larger than N of 1000 permuted-outcome draws"), described as such.
- **Per-gene-set p-values against both nulls**, BH-adjusted within the 50-set family, as
  the protocol's screen already specifies.
- The statement that the gene-set draw requirement (`min_null_draws: 1000`) is **met**.

**Not licensed by R1 alone:**

- **Tier A or B for any pathway.** That gate requires the draw count *and* a formally
  shown prediction gain — a one-sided 95% lower bound above zero. The lower bound needs
  the interval method that the deferred coverage simulation was to validate, so
  `primary_gain_shown` stays false and tiers stay capped at C.
- **A confidence interval** on ΔMAE. None has been validated for this design; do not
  construct one from the null draws, which describe the null, not the estimate.

**The one route that would change this, and its price.** A permutation test of the null
"expression adds nothing beyond tissue" is valid by construction under exchangeability of
the permuted outcome, and it needs no coverage simulation. Adopting it would give a
genuine p-value for the primary comparison and would let `primary_gain_shown` be decided.
But it is **not** the route the protocol prespecified (an interval method was), so:

> Adopting the permutation test requires a recorded amendment in
> `docs/protocol_amendments.md`, written **before** the null distribution is inspected,
> stating the test statistic (paired component-weighted ΔMAE), the null (outcome permuted
> within the analysis population), the draw count, the one-sided direction and the alpha.
> Written afterwards it is a post-hoc test chosen in sight of the result, and the report
> must then present it descriptively and say so.

`configs/experiment_controls.yaml` deliberately leaves `inference.method:
not_assessable`, so running R1 cannot silently convert the descriptive result into an
inferential one.

## 3. R2: leave-one-tissue-out

`configs/experiment_loto.yaml` holds out one tissue at a time (`tier: loto`, `repeats: 1`)
and fits M0 and **M2X**, the expression-only model. M2X rather than M2 is the right choice
here: when a whole tissue is held out, a tissue-effect term has no training data for it,
so comparing M2X against the global mean M0 asks the question cleanly — does expression
alone transfer to an unseen tissue?

- Report per-tissue MAE and the M0 → M2X difference, descriptively.
- Expect weak results, and report them as they come out. Eleven of 20 tissues have fewer
  than ten components, so most per-tissue estimates are unstable; say so rather than
  ranking them.
- This is **not** external validation: the cohort, labels and pipeline are unchanged.

## 4. R3: outcome-scale sensitivity

`configs/experiment_scales.yaml` repeats the M1-versus-M2 comparison on the `neglog` and
`logit` scales with 20 repeats. If the advantage is a scale artefact it should shrink or
reverse; if it is real but small it should persist in direction. Report the three scales
side by side without selecting the most favourable, and note that the raw scale remains
the protocol's primary scale whatever the others show.

## 5. R4: random forest M3, and the result that must be reported either way

`configs/experiment_exploratory_m3.yaml` fits M3 alongside M0, M1 and M2 on the identical
splits. It was configured **after** the primary results were seen, so:

- M3 **cannot become the primary model** whatever it scores;
- **if M3 performs worse than M2, that is the result and it is reported as such.** A
  tree ensemble underperforming a regularised linear offset on 237 components with 50
  coarse features is the expected outcome, and it is informative: it argues that the
  ceiling is set by the data, not by the model family.

## 6. R5: what is already covered without any fitting

| Area the investigator asked about | Where it is already handled |
|---|---|
| Cross-validation repeat stability | `per_repeat_delta_mae.csv`, Table S1, Figure 3c. Spread is split-to-split variability, labelled as such, never an interval |
| Tissue-level performance | `tissue_errors.csv`, Table 3, Figure 5; tissues below ten components excluded from the figure and flagged in the table |
| Calibration | `model_metrics.csv` calibration slope and intercept; Figure 4 shows the shrinkage directly |
| Generalisation | Honest statement that repeated CV is not external validation; R2 adds the only cheap real test |
| Within-tissue correlation artefact | **Avoided.** `within_tissue_spearman_v2` marks the statistic undefined where a model predicts a constant within a tissue, and biased downward elsewhere. The version-1 values are retained for audit and are **not interpreted anywhere** |

## 7. Analyses deliberately not recommended

| Not recommended | Why |
|---|---|
| Coverage simulation at full size | 9 × 500 replicates remains infeasible; this is the recorded D04.06 deviation and it stands |
| Bootstrap confidence intervals on ΔMAE | No bootstrap scheme has been validated for this nested, grouped, repeated design; constructing one would invent inference |
| Prospective prediction on unassayed DepMap lines | Needs identity QC of non-CLARIFi lines and a panel-selection rule written into the protocol before predictions are viewed (triage manifest records it as deferred). A designed panel is not an unbiased external cohort |
| Adding XGBoost or deep models | A new dependency and no distinct scientific question; M3 already tests whether a different family helps |
| Per-tissue explainability | Most lineages have too few components for stable importance estimates (recorded as deferred) |

## 8. Reporting rules that apply to all of the above

1. Any procedure whose validity has not been established for this design is reported
   **descriptively**, with the reason given.
2. No confidence interval, p-value or claim of independent validation is created that the
   executed analyses do not support.
3. Exploratory runs are named, versioned and separated from the frozen primary benchmark
   in every table, figure and sentence.
4. A result that weakens the project's story is reported with the same prominence as one
   that strengthens it.
